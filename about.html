The Spirit of "Hoichoi"

What Does "Hoichoi" Mean to Us?

In Bengali culture, "Hoichoi" (হৈচৈ) is an untranslatable emotion. It stands for the sweet commotion of lively laughter, fiery friendly debates over steaming tea, the clinking of spoons, and the warmth of sharing snacks across the table with your closest companions.

Located at 710 Shibpur Road, Chatterjeehut, Howrah, Hoichoi Cafe was founded with a singular dream: to give our local neighborhood a cozy, youthful, and vibrant sanctuary where good people could eat extraordinary food without worrying about exorbitant prices.

From students dropping in after classes to families sharing an evening pizza, we have grown into one of Howrah's most beloved hangout destinations, earning a proud 4.1 rating over 126+ verified Google reviews.

What We Excel At

Our Culinary Specialities

The Atmosphere

Intimate, Cozy & Filled with Warmth

As our guests often point out in their reviews, while our space is cozy and intimate, that compactness is precisely what gives Hoichoi Cafe its warm, close-knit charm. You can smell the freshly melted cheese and stone-baked pizza crusts right from your seat.

Soft amber lighting casts a relaxing glow over our tables, gentle music sets the background rhythm, and comfortable seating makes you feel right at home. Guests typically spend between 10 to 45 minutes here catching up over snacks, discussing university projects, or taking a quick mindful break.
