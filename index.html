Welcome to Hoichoi Cafe

More Than Just a Café — A Place for Lively Memories.

In Bengal, "Hoichoi" translates to joyful commotion, cheerful laughter, and unforgettable banter with friends and family. Situated at Chatterjeehut along Shibpur Road in Howrah, Hoichoi Cafe was built to give the neighborhood a warm, welcoming space to recharge.

Whether you are catching up over our signature Cheese Baked Momos, biting into a crunchy Chicken Popcorn basket, or sipping a chilled Blue Heaven Mocktail, every minute here is filled with comfort and good energy.
