Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dining at Hoichoi Cafe.

Is there any minimum bill amount?

Not at all! You are welcome whether you are ordering a single cup of Special Tea (₹49) or a full Chinese Combo feast. The average spend reported by guests is ₹200–400 per person.

Can we bring our own birthday cake?

Yes! We love celebrations. You can bring your own cake, and our friendly staff will happily assist with knife, plates, and candles at no extra charge.

When is the café least crowded?

Around 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM, the café is usually calm and relaxed — perfect for a peaceful lunch, reading, or quiet conversation. Evenings (5 PM – 9 PM) are bustling and energetic.

How do I modify or cancel my reservation?
